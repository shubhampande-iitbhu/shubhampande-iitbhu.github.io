I am an Assistant Professor in the [Department of Electronics Engineering](https://www.iitbhu.ac.in/dept/ece) at [IIT (BHU) Varanasi](https://www.iitbhu.ac.in/). Previously, I was a Device Modeling Engineer at [Intel Technology India](https://www.intel.in/), Hyderabad, where I worked on PDK development for the 14A/10A process nodes.

I received my Direct PhD (MS + PhD) from [IIT Madras](https://www.iitm.ac.in/) in 2025, advised by [Prof. Anjan Chakravorty](https://www.ee.iitm.ac.in/anjan/) and [Prof. Bhaswar Chakrabarti](https://sites.google.com/view/end-ip-lab/home). My thesis focused on ReRAM Memory Technology for Neuromorphic Computing — spanning device design, compact modelling, and circuit-level applications. I was a [Fulbright-Nehru Doctoral Research Fellow](https://www.usief.org.in/meet-our-india-fulbr/shubham-pande/) at [UC Santa Barbara](https://www.ucsb.edu/) (2022–23), where I worked with [Prof. Dmitri Strukov](https://sites.google.com/site/strukov/) synaptic devices for online training of spiking neural networks.

## Research Interests

- Emerging Memories — ReRAM, FeFET, and DRAM for storage and in-memory computing
- Emerging Computing Paradigms — neuromorphic hardware, reservoir computing, compute-in-memory
- Advanced CMOS Devices and Design–Technology Co-Optimization (DTCO)
